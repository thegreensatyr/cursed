Blood Elf & the Order of the Stone

Part One · Cursed Worlds, Book One

Being ghosted by the man he loves is the least of Prince Reed's worries. He wakes in a dungeon with an altered appearance and an insatiable thirst for blood — turned by the vampires who attacked him.

When his mentor, the cunning man Tullot, is accused of conspiring with those same vampires and sentenced to die, Reed sets out to clear his name — though Tullot refuses to defend himself. Reed's blood lust threatens the fae kingdom, and he is banished to the human realm, forced to turn to Cernn, the warrior who broke his heart, and the misfit team under his command.

In New York City's supernatural underworld, Reed must rely on one of his own attackers to teach him control, if he is ever to go home and win back the man he loves. Instead, a power struggle inside the vampire hierarchy pulls him into manipulation, betrayal, and hidden agendas that threaten his life and his kingdom — while mysteries long buried surface, and an ancient enemy returns for revenge.

The Author

B. Xen Osiris Zane

Actor, musician, DJ, filmmaker — and, since you're reading this, an author too. He writes Supernatural Fantasy and Dark Comedy, with stories built around strong LGBTQ+ characters.

Cursed Worlds began as a screenplay. It was rewritten so many times, at the direction of so many conflicting industry notes, that it eventually came back unrecognizable from what he'd started with. So he scrapped the drafts, went back to the original concept, and wrote it as a novel instead.

He also writes outside the genre. This Marine's American Dream was a ghostwritten autobiography. Obligations, a memoir, arrives in 2027.
