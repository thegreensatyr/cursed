## 🔑 THE THREE THINGS THAT MAKE THIS WINNABLE

**1. Kindle Unlimited means "buy" is free.**
Your ask isn't "spend $5.99 on an unknown author." For a KU subscriber it's "tap this, it costs nothing." That is the lowest-friction conversion in publishing, and you get paid on pages read regardless. **Every CTA in this funnel leads with "Free in Kindle Unlimited," not with the price.**

**2. External traffic now boosts your Amazon ranking.**
As of 2026 Amazon's algorithm gives a ranking lift to books receiving traffic from *outside* Amazon. So the funnel does double duty: it sells copies, and the act of sending outside traffic raises where you rank organically. Doing nothing keeps you at #2.6M forever.

**3. You have genuinely good art.**
The banner, the emblem, the cover — this is a visual-first genre and your assets are strong. Most indie dark fantasy looks worse than yours. Lead with images everywhere.

## STAGE 1 — LANDING PAGE

**URL:** `cursedworlds.com/read` — built, in this folder as `read.html`

Deliberately stripped: no navigation, no footer links, no "about." One decision on the page. Every extra link is a leak.

**The offer:** the first three chapters of Blood Elf, free, by email.

Why chapters and not a quiz or a prequel: it costs you nothing to make — the material already exists — and it ships this week. A "Which of the Cursed are you?" quiz would out-share it and is worth building as phase two, but don't build two things before either is live.

**Why email and not a direct Amazon link:** a click to Amazon is a one-time event you never see again. An email address is an asset you own and can use in November. Send cold traffic to the list, not to the storefront.

## STAGE 3 — TRACKING (do this before you spend a dollar on ads)

Once someone clicks through to Amazon, you go blind — unless you set up **Amazon Attribution**, which is free and available to KDP authors.

It gives you click → purchase (and KU borrow) data on external traffic, with a 14-day attribution window, so you can actually tell which post or email produced sales instead of guessing.

**Setup:** `advertising.amazon.com` → sign in with your KDP credentials → **Measurement & Reporting** → **Amazon Attribution** → create a tag per channel (one for email, one for Facebook, one for the landing page).

Without this you are spending money into a black box. With it you know within a week what's working.

**Everything else you can watch for free:**

| Metric | Where | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page → email | Netlify Forms | 30%+ |
| Email open rate | your email tool | 40%+ (fresh list) |
| Email 3 click rate | your email tool | 15%+ |
| Amazon rank | the product page | any movement under #500k is real traction |
| Reviews | product page | **first target: 10** |

## STAGE 4 — TRAFFIC

Order matters. Do the free things first; they tell you whether the funnel converts before you pay for traffic.

**Week 1 — free**
- Point cursedworlds.com at the new site *(kills the ad-parking leak — this alone is a fix)*
- Post the emblem + cover to your Facebook Page, link to `/read`
- Update the Amazon bio to link the site
- Update satyrmoon.com to link the live site rather than a dead one

**Week 2–3 — free, higher effort**
- The banner art as a series of posts — one character at a time, each ending at `/read`
- Your author story (email 4) as a public post. It's your strongest piece of writing and it's true.
- Any LGBTQ+ fantasy / dark fantasy reader groups you're already in — participate, don't spam

**Week 4+ — paid, only after the funnel converts**
- Facebook traffic ads to `/read`, art-led, $5–10/day to test
- Amazon Ads on the product page once you have ~10 reviews (before that, ads convert badly)

**Do not run paid traffic until the landing page is converting above 25%.** Paying to send people into a leaky funnel is the most common way indie authors lose money.

## THE PART THAT MATTERS MOST

You have **zero reviews**, and that's the single biggest thing holding this book down — more than price, cover, or blurb. Amazon's machinery barely surfaces a book with no reviews, and Amazon Ads won't perform against one.

**Getting to 10 reviews changes the economics of everything else in this document.**

The fastest legitimate route: your free-chapter list. People who read three chapters and then the book are exactly who leaves reviews, and email 5 asks them directly. This is another reason the funnel builds a list rather than pushing straight to the storefront.

Never pay for reviews or trade them — Amazon detects it and it can get a book delisted.
